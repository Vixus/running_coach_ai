Your Magazine Story

Every athlete has a story worth telling. As you train, your coach will quietly capture the moments worth narrating.

Questions When It Matters

After a PR, an upcoming race, or a tough week, your coach asks a few short questions in chat — multiple-choice or your own words.

Your Words Shape The Story

When a milestone arrives — your goal race, a season finale — your coach uses everything you've shared to write a 400–600 word editorial in a magazine style.

Pick Your Style

The system picks a magazine template that matches your story's voice. You can swap to any of the five — Vogue-style, Runner's World, Times Long Read, Outside, GQ — instantly.

Private Until You Share

Your story is private until you press Share. Then it becomes a public link you can send to anyone — and you can unpublish or delete it any time.
